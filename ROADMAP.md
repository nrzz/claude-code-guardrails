# Roadmap

What could come next. None of it is promised; all of it is open to contributors. Comment on an issue or start a [discussion](https://github.com/nrzz/claude-code-guardrails/discussions) before a large change.

- Ask before more publish commands: `mix hex.publish`, `dart pub publish` and `flutter pub publish`, `mvn deploy`, `gradle publish` and `helm push` (`npm`, `cargo`, `twine`, `gem`, `poetry` and others are covered).
- Per-folder presets (stricter inside `infra/`).
- `claude-guardrails explain <rule>` with examples of what the rule catches and lets through.
